Trade in what you no longer use

Bring your old art materials to the FabLab, Nov 2 to 20

How to join raffle:

  1. Gather the art materials you no longer use.
  2. Bring them to FabLab, November 2 to 20, 1:00 to 4:30 PM, weekdays only, no weekends.
  3. Follow the Ubay-Ubay page. The organizers check each item and assigns its entry value.
  4. Write your name and contact on the entry slip.

Final valuation is up to the organizers.

Entry values

Not every item is worth the same. A full sketchbook goes further than one pencil.

1 entry: single pencils, pens, markers, erasers, rulers, brushes

2 entries: crayon and colored pencil sets, small paint sets, glue and tape, paper bundles

3 entries: notebooks, sketchbooks, large paint sets, canvases, yarn bundles

What we accept:

  • Pencils
  • Colored Pencils
  • Crayons
  • Markers
  • Yarn
  • Paint
  • Paintbrushes
  • Watercolor
  • Sketchbooks
  • Notebooks
  • Drawing Paper
  • Rulers
  • Erasers
  • Sharpeners
  • Glue
  • Tape
  • Unused or partly used canvases

We cannot take broken or damaged items, empty or dried out pens, markers, and paints, anything unclean or unsafe

Items have to be good enough that another student would want to use them.
